1. General

This Policy describes how the zh-gallery website (the “Site”) processes and protects the personal data of its users. It is based on Federal Law No. 152-FZ of 27 July 2006 “On Personal Data” of the Russian Federation.

The data operator is the owner of the Site (the “Operator”). Contact: zhenya@zh-gallery.ru.

2. What data is processed

  • e-mail address and username;
  • password — stored only as an irreversible hash; the plain password is never kept;
  • data received when signing in with Google: name, e-mail address, account identifier (only if you press “Continue with Google” yourself);
  • activity on the Site: comments, likes, registration date;
  • technical data: IP address, cookies, browser details and request time.

3. Purposes of processing

  • registration, authentication and account management;
  • publishing comments and displaying likes;
  • replying to user requests;
  • keeping the Site secure and preventing abuse;
  • depersonalised traffic statistics.

4. Legal grounds

Processing is based on Article 6(1) of Law No. 152-FZ:

  • consent of the data subject (item 1) — given at registration;
  • performance of a contract to which the subject is a party (item 5) — the Terms of Use;
  • legitimate interests of the Operator regarding the security of the Site (item 7).

5. How data is processed

Processing uses databases located in the territory of the Russian Federation (Article 18(5) of Law No. 152-FZ). Data is not shared with third parties except:

  • Google as the sign-in provider, when the corresponding button is used;
  • the hosting provider that runs the Site.

No cross-border transfer of personal data is performed. Data is kept until the account is deleted or consent is withdrawn.

6. Cookies

The Site uses cookies to keep the session and remember the chosen language. You can disable them in your browser, but signing in and commenting will stop working.

7. Your rights

You may request information about the processing of your data, ask for it to be corrected, blocked or deleted, and withdraw your consent. Requests: zhenya@zh-gallery.ru. A reply is provided within 30 days (Article 20 of Law No. 152-FZ); data is deleted within 30 days of the request (Article 21 of Law No. 152-FZ).

8. Security measures

Server access is restricted, the connection is protected with HTTPS, passwords are hashed, regular backups are taken and access rights are separated.

9. Changes to this Policy

The current version is always available on this page. Material changes are announced the next time the user visits the Site.

10. Contacts

For any questions about personal data: zhenya@zh-gallery.ru.
